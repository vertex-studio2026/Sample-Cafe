Our Story

More Than Coffee — It's a Ritual

Born from a passion for extraordinary coffee, Maison Roast opened its doors in 2014 with one simple belief: that a truly great cup of coffee can change your day. We source single-origin beans from family-owned farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala — then roast them in-house to unlock their full potential.

Our cozy, sunlit space was designed to be your second home — a place to connect, create, and be present. Every detail, from our hand-thrown ceramics to our seasonal pastry menu, is chosen with intention and love.

The People

Meet the Artisans

Our passionate team brings years of craft, creativity, and genuine warmth to every cup and every plate.

Étienne Moreau

Head Barista & Co-Founder

Champion barista with 15 years of experience. Étienne trained in Milan and Tokyo before founding Maison Roast with a vision to bring world-class espresso to the community.

Clara & Yumi

Lead Pastry Chefs

Our dynamic duo behind every laminated dough and seasonal tart. Clara trained at Le Cordon Bleu; Yumi brings her Japanese precision and love of subtle flavour to every bake.

Tariq Hassan

Head Roaster & Bean Sourcer

Tariq travels the world's coffee belt twice yearly to hand-select our green beans directly from farmers. His roast profiles have won multiple specialty coffee awards.
