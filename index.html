Our Story

Where Every Dish Tells a Story

Founded in 2009, Lumière was born from a dream — to create a space where food transcends the ordinary and becomes an unforgettable experience. Our Executive Chef, Antoine Moreau, brings together the traditions of classical French cuisine with bold, contemporary flavors sourced from around the globe.

We believe dining is an art form. Every plate is a canvas, every ingredient a brushstroke, and every meal a masterpiece crafted with love and precision just for you.
